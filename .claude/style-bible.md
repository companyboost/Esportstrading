- Logo: assets/logo/esports-trading.svg (client pick: original bolt-cut yellow E, white SPORTS, yellow TRADING tag). Source: ../eSport Trading League/branding/final/main-logo/V1-yellow-E-transparent.svg

- Website reference layout: ~/Downloads/Esports Trading Website.pdf (client's own mockup, layout only, not style)

## Hard rules (never violate)
- Logo is the ORIGINAL bolt-cut E (V1). Do not use the E-options replacements (John, 2026-09-26).
- Never use em dashes anywhere.
- No Arial, Inter, Roboto or system fonts. Site uses Chakra Petch (display) + Instrument Sans (body).
- Generated images must not look like AI slop: no neon overload, no holograms, no hexagon grids, no floating glowing UI, no glossy plastic skin, no text or fake logos baked into images.
- Photography direction: real people, real arenas, editorial sports photography, cinema camera look, film grain, shallow depth of field, moody near-black with ONE acid-yellow accent light. Restrained, not garish.
- No cat, no FatCat mascot in Esports Trading imagery. EXCEPTION (John, 2026-09-26): the FatCat Arena partner logo must always be shown WITH the cat-face icon (assets/partners/fatcat-icon-white.svg + wordmark).

## Look and feel
- Mood: mainstream sports league meets trading terminal. Futuristic, animated, bold, trustworthy.
- Palette: near-black #0A0A0B, acid yellow #EDFC0D, white #F4F4F2. Loss red #FF3B5C only as a chart accent.
- Motion: Three.js arena hero, staggered reveals, slanted (skewX -8deg) buttons and tags.

## Defaults
- Image model: seedream-5-pro (photorealistic). recraft-v4-1 for vector/illustration.
- Aspect ratios: hero/closing 21:9, section photos 3:4 or 4:5, cards 4:5, blog covers 16:9.
- Batch size: 1 per slot when filling a site; 4 variants only when exploring a single hero.
- Save downloaded images to assets/img/ and record ids below.
